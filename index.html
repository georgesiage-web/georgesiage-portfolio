About

Media with a purpose

I’ve always seen media as more than cameras, editing or technology. For me, it is a way to share the love of Jesus.

Through livestreaming, content creation and digital communications, I use media to support the work of the gospel. A message shared on a screen can reach someone who might never walk into a church, and that is why this work matters to me.

I want to keep learning and growing, and to use the gifts God has given me to tell others about Jesus, encourage the Church and help take the everlasting gospel to the world.

Music in mission

I’m a gospel singer and choir director who loves sharing Jesus through music. I sing with Breath of Praise and Mission Trio, have served with B. Heritage, and value music that ministers to hearts. At Purelight Missions I trained and directed the choir, growing its musicianship while using music to evangelise. I want my gifts to glorify God and lead others to Christ.

Working together

I work on contract, so every project is built around your team, your goals and your timeline.

I join your team

I can lead as producer or fill the role you need, whether that is shooting, editing, sound or script. I share drafts early and welcome your notes.

I adapt to your setup

New software, different cameras, an existing workflow. I learn what your project runs on and work inside it, so your pipeline stays as it is.

I start from your message

I begin with what you want people to understand and feel. The script, shots and sound follow from that.

Happy to share what I know

I enjoy passing on what I have learned, in music (singing and choir training) and in media production (scripting, shooting, editing and sound). I can coach one person or work alongside your team.
